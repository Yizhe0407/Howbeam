# Howbeam Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-10-18

[English](#english) · [繁體中文](#繁體中文)

---

## English

Howbeam is a browser extension that records the clicks you make on a web page and turns them into a step-by-step guide with screenshots. This policy explains what data Howbeam handles and what it does with it.

### Summary

Everything Howbeam records stays in your browser on your computer. Howbeam has no account, no server and no analytics. It does not send your data anywhere, and it does not sell or share it.

### What Howbeam handles

Howbeam only handles data while you are recording, on the tabs that are part of a recording you started:

- **Screenshots** of the visible part of the page. A screenshot shows everything visible on the page at that moment, which can include personal information if the page displays it.
- **The page address (URL)** of each recorded page.
- **The visible text of the element you click**, or its label, placeholder or accessible name. Howbeam does not read the values you type into form fields, but a screenshot shows them if they are visible on screen.
- **Position and timing**: where the element is on the screen, the window size and scroll position, and when the click happened.

Howbeam also keeps:

- **What you create in the editor**: descriptions, annotations and project titles.
- **Your settings**: interface language, recording preferences, and the folder you chose for automatic backup.

### Where it is stored

- Projects are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your computer. Settings and the state of a recording in progress are stored in the extension's local storage.
- If you turn on automatic backup, Howbeam writes `.howbeam` files to the folder you choose. If that folder is synced by a cloud service, that service's own policy applies to the files.
- When you export, the file is saved where you choose.

### What Howbeam does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or any third-party service.
- It does not sell, rent or share data.
- It does not run on pages you are not recording.

### Permissions

- **activeTab, scripting**: run the recorder in the tab you choose to record and take screenshots of it.
- **Access to all sites (optional)**: asked for only when you start a Steps recording, so recording can continue when you switch tabs or sites. You can decline and record only the current tab.
- **storage, unlimitedStorage**: keep your projects and settings on your computer.
- **downloads**: save the files you export.
- **clipboardWrite**: copy a step's image when you ask for it.

### Keeping and deleting your data

- Deleting a project in the library removes it from the browser.
- Uninstalling Howbeam removes all of its data from the browser.
- Backup and export files are yours. Howbeam never deletes them, so remove them yourself if you no longer want them.
- `.howbeam` files contain the original screenshots without masks. Keep them private.

### Changes to this policy

If the way Howbeam handles data changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the change will be announced in the extension.

### Contact

Questions about this policy: <https://github.com/Yizhe0407/Stepmark/issues>

## 繁體中文

Howbeam 是一個瀏覽器擴充功能，會記錄你在網頁上的點擊，轉成附截圖的逐步教學。本政策說明 Howbeam 處理哪些資料，以及如何處理。

### 摘要

Howbeam 記錄的所有資料都只存在你電腦上的瀏覽器裡。Howbeam 沒有帳號、沒有伺服器，也沒有分析工具。它不會把你的資料傳到任何地方，也不會出售或分享。

### Howbeam 處理的資料

Howbeam 只在你錄製時，針對你開始錄製的分頁處理資料：

- **截圖**：頁面上目前看得到的範圍。截圖會拍下當下頁面上所有看得到的內容；如果頁面顯示個人資訊，也會被拍到。
- **網址**：每個被錄製頁面的網址。
- **被點擊元素上的文字**：或它的標籤、提示文字（placeholder）、無障礙名稱。Howbeam 不會讀取你在表單欄位裡輸入的值，但如果那些字顯示在畫面上，截圖會拍到。
- **位置與時間**：元素在畫面上的位置、視窗大小與捲動位置，以及點擊的時間。

Howbeam 也會保存：

- **你在編輯器裡製作的內容**：說明文字、標註、專案名稱。
- **你的設定**：介面語言、錄製偏好，以及你選來自動備份的資料夾。

### 存放位置

- 專案存在你電腦上瀏覽器的本機儲存空間（IndexedDB）。設定與正在進行中的錄製狀態，存在擴充功能的本機儲存空間。
- 開啟自動備份時，Howbeam 會把 `.howbeam` 檔寫到你選的資料夾。如果這個資料夾由雲端服務同步，這些檔案適用該服務自己的政策。
- 匯出時，檔案會存到你選的位置。

### Howbeam 不會做的事

- 不會把任何資料傳給開發者或其他任何人。
- 不使用分析、廣告或任何第三方服務。
- 不出售、出租或分享資料。
- 不會在你沒有錄製的頁面上執行。

### 權限

- **activeTab、scripting**：在你選擇錄製的分頁執行錄製程式並截圖。
- **存取所有網站（選用）**：只在你開始「步驟」錄製時詢問，讓你切換分頁或網站時能繼續錄製。你可以拒絕，只錄製目前分頁。
- **storage、unlimitedStorage**：把專案與設定存在你的電腦上。
- **downloads**：儲存你匯出的檔案。
- **clipboardWrite**：在你要求時複製步驟圖片。

### 保留與刪除

- 在專案庫刪除專案，就會從瀏覽器中移除。
- 解除安裝 Howbeam，會移除它在瀏覽器裡的所有資料。
- 備份檔與匯出檔屬於你，Howbeam 不會刪除它們；不需要時請自行刪除。
- `.howbeam` 檔含有未遮罩的原始截圖，請妥善保管。

### 政策變更

如果 Howbeam 處理資料的方式改變，會在新版本發布前更新本政策，並在擴充功能中告知。

### 聯絡方式

對本政策有疑問，請到：<https://github.com/Yizhe0407/Stepmark/issues>
